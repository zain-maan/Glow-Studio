## Project overview

The studio took every booking over WhatsApp and phone. Clients waited hours for a reply, stylists were double-booked, and the owner had no clear picture of revenue or how full the chairs were.

The new website shows real availability and lets clients build a basket of services, pick a specialist or "anyone", and choose from genuinely free times. When a booking needs more than one specialist, the system schedules them back to back automatically. Every booking lands instantly in the owner's dashboard, which shows today's revenue and schedule, a calendar per stylist, a searchable client list with spend and history, and team performance, with one-click confirm, cancel and reschedule.

## Key features

1. **Live availability**: only real free slots are shown, with the next free one right in the hero.
2. **Smart multi-specialist booking**: hair and nails in one visit, scheduled back to back with the right people.
3. **Tap-to-add price list**: a floating basket with running time and total follows clients across the site.
4. **Owner dashboard**: revenue, bookings, occupancy and trends at a glance.
5. **Stylist calendar**: every appointment by stylist and hour, with reschedule in two taps.
6. **Client CRM**: visits, spend, VIPs and full history for every client.
